About me

Hi, I am Maya. I am a first-year web design student, and I study online while working part time at a library.

I got interested in the web when I tried to renew a library book on a phone and could not tap the right button. That made me want to build pages that work for everyone, whatever device or ability they have.

This year I have learned HTML, CSS, and a little JavaScript. Next, I want to learn more about accessible forms and design systems.

What I can do

  • Build

    Semantic HTML, responsive CSS with Flexbox and Grid, and small JavaScript features.

  • Check

    Accessibility scans with WAVE, keyboard testing, and testing at phone and desktop widths.

  • Share

    Publishing with GitHub Pages and keeping a README that explains my choices.
